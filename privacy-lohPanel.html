Privacy Policy

At LohPanel (the admin control panel for Lohomna), we value your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect from administrators and how it is used.

Information We Collect

When you use LohPanel, we collect only the following information:

  • Name – to identify the administrator.
  • Phone number – used for login (OTP verification) and for essential communications.

We do not collect email addresses, government IDs, or financial information.

How We Use Your Information

Your name and phone number are used only for:

  • Granting access to the admin panel (authentication).
  • Contacting you if necessary regarding panel updates or security matters.

Data Accessed Through the Panel

As an administrator, you may view customer names and phone numbers (collected by the Lohomna app) in order to process orders. You are strictly prohibited from using this data for any purpose other than fulfilling orders and customer support.

Data Security & Sharing

We store your name and phone number securely and do not share them with any third party. No payment or sensitive data is stored in LohPanel.

Your Rights

You may request a copy of your data (name and phone number) or ask for corrections by contacting us. If you no longer need access to LohPanel, you can request deletion of your admin account.
